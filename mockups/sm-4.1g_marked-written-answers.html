7.  State Ohm's law and give one condition under which it holds.

Correct · 3 of 3
Ohm's law says the current through a conductor is proportional to the voltage across it. It holds when the temperature stays the same.
A full answer includes: the proportional relationship, and constant temperature. Clear and complete.

8.  Two resistors of 6 Ω and 3 Ω are connected in parallel across a 12 V battery. Calculate the total resistance and the current supplied.

Partly correct · 1 of 3
R = 6 + 3 = 9 Ω, so I = 12 / 9 = 1.33 A.
A full answer includes: 1/R = 1/6 + 1/3, so R = 2 Ω; then I = 12 / 2 = 6 A. You added the resistors as if they were in series. In parallel, add their reciprocals.

9.  Explain why household appliances are connected in parallel rather than in series.

Correct · 2 of 2
So each one gets the full voltage and you can switch one off without turning off the others.
A full answer includes: full supply voltage to each, and independent switching. Good: both reasons given.

10.  What happens to the current if the resistance is doubled and the voltage stays the same? Explain why.

Missed · 0 of 2
The current doubles because there is more resistance.
A full answer includes: the current halves, because I = V / R. More resistance means less current, not more.
